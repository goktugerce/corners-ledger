How bets are picked and settled
  • v1: negative-binomial corner total × beta-binomial home share, priced against every Pinnacle corner line and Iddaa's .5 lines, plus Iddaa's team totals (.5 lines), most corners (1/0/2), total range and “both teams at least k corners” (no Pinnacle equivalent, so no CLV). "New leagues" is the same model refitted with Scotland, League One and Two, Greece, Switzerland, Denmark, Sweden, MLS, Argentina, Japan and Mexico; only those leagues' bets are shown there.
  • A bet is logged the first time a fixture's market clears 3% model edge at odds 1.70–2.30; one bet per fixture and market. Iddaa: singles-only (MBS 1) markets.
  • Settled from FotMob's final corner counts. Quarter lines (9.25, −1.75) are two half-stakes; a whole line that lands exactly is a push.
  • CLV: odds taken × Pinnacle's no-vig closing probability − 1; break-even is 0%. The close is the recorder's last Pinnacle price before kickoff (polled every 10 minutes), or the OddsNotifier bot for older bets.
  • Vs posted close: odds taken ÷ Pinnacle's posted closing odds − 1. The posted price keeps Pinnacle's margin, so break-even is roughly that margin (about +5%).
